Climate Haven Radars

Logan, UT is the baseline — where we are now. The default Δ vs Logan view puts Logan on the bold center ring; every other city bows outward where it beats Logan and inward where it’s worse, one axis at a time, for a family’s 30s–40s (the window when Logan degrades). Five radars — Comfort 2030s, Comfort 2040s, Economic, Accessibility, Safety — cover safety, outdoor comfort, access (what makes life possible), and amenities (what makes it enjoyable). Compare the two Comfort tabs to spot places that fade by the 2040s. Flip to Absolute 0–10 for raw grades. Grades are locked; hit Unlock editing to change values or add a city. Click a numbered vertex for its label; click a city in the legend to isolate it, click another to morph, double-click to show all.
