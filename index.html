Climate Haven Radars

Two star charts for the move decision, scored on an absolute national scale — each grade ≈ a percentile among viable US family-raising places (a 7 ≈ better than ~70% of them). Two summary rows: Overall = average percentile; Balance = radar area, which punishes any single terrible axis. Grades are locked; hit Unlock editing to change values or add a city. Click a numbered vertex to pop its label. Click a city in the legend to isolate its star; click another to morph between them; double-click to show all. Use the checkbox by each city to hide it. Cells shade green at 8+ and red at 3−. Higher is always better.
